\# Provenance and Licensing Policy



\## Purpose



This repository contains computational reproductions of published scientific

results. Its purpose is to make numerical methods, implementations,

computational results, and validation procedures traceable and independently

checkable.



The repository distinguishes between:



1\. the original scientific work being reproduced;

2\. the independent numerical formulation and implementation developed for the

&#x20;  reproduction;

3\. computational results generated by the reproduction;

4\. documentation describing the reproduction and its validation; and

5\. software and other material originating from third parties.



\## Original Scientific Publications



The scientific papers reproduced by this repository remain the intellectual

property of their respective authors, publishers, or other rights holders,

subject to the applicable terms governing those works.



This repository does not claim ownership of the original publications or of

material reproduced from them.



References to published equations, numerical results, figures, terminology,

and other scientific content are provided for purposes of scientific

reproducibility, attribution, and verification.



Users should consult the original publication and its applicable rights and

licensing terms before reusing material originating from that publication.



\## Independent Numerical Implementations



The computational implementations in this repository are intended to provide

independent numerical implementations of published scientific results.



An implementation may use a numerical method that differs from the method used

in the original publication. The reproduction documentation describes the

mathematical formulation, numerical method, boundary and initial conditions,

computational procedures, and validation criteria used by the independent

implementation.



The repository does not claim that an independent implementation is identical

to the original authors' software or computational procedure.



\## AI-Assisted Development



Artificial-intelligence tools may be used during the development of

implementations, documentation, debugging, numerical experimentation, and

other research-support activities.



AI assistance does not by itself determine the scientific validity of a

reproduction. The responsibility for selecting the numerical formulation,

specifying the computational problem, evaluating the implementation,

executing the calculations, interpreting the results, and documenting the

validation remains with the researcher responsible for the reproduction.



AI-assisted code is therefore treated as computational implementation whose

scientific behavior must be independently examined and validated.



Where appropriate, repository documentation should identify substantial use of

AI assistance so that the provenance of the implementation remains

transparent.



\## Verification Principle



The primary criterion for a successful reproduction is quantitative agreement

with the published scientific result under a documented and independently

checkable computational procedure.



The repository therefore emphasizes:



\- traceability from published equations to executable implementation;

\- explicit documentation of numerical methods;

\- reproducible computational environments;

\- independently generated numerical results;

\- quantitative comparison with published results; and

\- transparent reporting of discrepancies and limitations.



The use of AI-assisted development does not replace these requirements.



\## Copyright and Licensing



Unless a specific file or directory states otherwise, this repository should

not be interpreted as granting rights to third-party material contained in or

referenced by the repository.



The copyright status of AI-generated material may depend on the nature and

extent of human contribution and on applicable law. This repository therefore

does not make a blanket claim that every line of AI-assisted code is

copyrightable by the repository authors.



Where a separate software license is subsequently applied to original

repository material, that license will govern only the material to which it

legally applies and will not be intended to relicense third-party material or

the original scientific publications.



\## Attribution



Reproductions should clearly identify the original scientific publication and

provide the appropriate bibliographic reference.



Independent implementations should also identify the computational method and

the provenance of the implementation sufficiently for another researcher to

understand how the reproduced result was obtained.



\## Reproducibility and Transparency



A reproduction is intended to be evaluated on the basis of its documented

mathematical formulation, executable implementation, computational results,

and quantitative validation.



The repository favors transparency over claims of authorship that cannot be

clearly established. AI-assisted development is disclosed as part of the

computational provenance rather than treated as a substitute for numerical

verification.
